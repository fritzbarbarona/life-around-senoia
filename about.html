About the Publication

A magazine written by the town, for the town.

Life Around Senoia is more than a magazine — it's a celebration of community, connection, and the people who make this small town special.

"We're both fathers, friends, and participants in our communities. This publication is our way of giving back — of highlighting what's good, celebrating what's real, and making sure no one in town feels like a stranger."

— Kevin Thompson, Publisher
Our Team

Who's behind the pages

Kevin Thompson

Publisher & Founder

Nearly 20 years ago, Kevin got his start in publishing working with a niche publisher of nearly 35 enthusiast titles — a journey that eventually led him to launch a business focused on supporting publishers and content-driven organizations, helping them scale their operations, tell better stories, and grow their communities.

Blake Adams

Advertising Director & Managing Partner

Born and raised in Fayette and Coweta County, Blake has proudly called Senoia home for over 15 years. He's been an active part of the local business and creative scene, with a deep passion for connecting people and building things that matter to his hometown.
