The Brewington Family
Issue 6
Issue 6 · Featured Family

Making Room at the Table

Spend a few minutes with Darrin, Ashley, and their nearly two-year-old daughter Nayeli, and their warmth is contagious. Both Georgia natives, they grew up in Fayetteville, married in 2019, and met working at Chick-fil-A — starting on the very same day. They chose Senoia because it placed them right between both sides of their family.

Darrin's path led from Worship Pastor at New Vision Church to Newnan City Church, where the Coweta tornado reshaped how he saw ministry — the most meaningful moments happening in living rooms and around dinner tables. Today he's planting Abide Church in Senoia, with a soft launch this August and an official launch on January 10, 2027, while Ashley serves patients as an oncology nurse. The belief behind it all: everyone deserves to know there's a place for them.

"Community isn't something you stumble upon — it's something you intentionally create."
The Jenkins Family

Issue 4 · Featured Family

The Jenkins Family Is All In

Stephen and Katelyn Jenkins are ALL IN on life. They show up in big ways — for each other, for their son, for their friends, for their church, and for this community they proudly call home.

Since moving to Senoia from Thomaston in 2018, they haven't just lived here — they've leaned in, drawn by the town's charm and a search for a next chapter closer to family and easier airport access.

A Family Whose Values Helped Shape a Town — and Still Do

Some families leave a mark on a town. Others become part of its foundation. In Senoia, the Crook family is the latter — a name woven quietly but deeply into the character of the community.

For Ellis Crook, those values weren't abstract ideas. They were a way of life, passed down from his father Arry Lee: hard work, integrity, faith, and service.

Issue 6 · Young Achiever

Shocking the World, One Race at a Time — Milo Stupski

At first glance, nine-year-old Milo Stupski might seem a little quiet — until you put him on a dirt bike. Known on the circuit as the "Hole Shot King," Milo competes in the Grand National Cross Country (GNCC) Series and currently sits ranked No. 3 in the nation in the Micro-E division. With six victories and counting, he lives by the three words written across his handlebars: Shock the World. His advice to other kids? "Don't give up. Be brave."
